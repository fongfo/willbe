Technical details
Pusaka hashes a summary of your plan (a Merkle root) and writes the hash, timestamp, and version number to a "Proof of Plan" smart contract on the Polygon network. Your raw plan data is encrypted and stored off-chain — it is never written to the public record, and nothing here can be used to identify you or your assets.
This proves your plan hasn't been silently altered. It does not expose any of your private information — only a mathematical fingerprint of it.
